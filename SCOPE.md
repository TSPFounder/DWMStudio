# SCOPE.md — Dream World Maker MVP

**Target:** Playable vertical slice by **September 1, 2026**
**Engine:** Unreal Engine 5.3 (both DWM and DWM_Dev for the MVP)
**Rule:** The date is fixed. Fidelity flexes. Nothing moves from *Out* to *In* without a dated entry in the Decisions Log below, stating what is being cut to make room.

---

## Definition of Done

The MVP is shipped when a person can, without developer intervention:

1. Load the DWM world in UE 5.3 and move an avatar through five distinct communities (Mountain, Hillside, Valley, Suburb, City).
2. See each community's headline resources and current Stone (St) balance on screen, sourced from the authoritative SQLite ledger.
3. Execute at least one cross-community trade that mints Stone correctly (buyer negative, seller positive, network total = 0).
4. Watch a Dollar Vault deplete and a Cascading Failure warning state trigger when a vault is exhausted.
5. Observe one engineered system promoted through DWM_Dev → verification gate → DWM (the Mountain wind-turbine, driven for the MVP by the **R2011a Simulink turbine model** — see the 2026-08-02 entry; the Simscape-physics option is now a post-MVP upgrade and the analytic fallback below is no longer needed).

> argument still holds and the honesty clause at the end applies MORE
> forcefully now, not less — see the 2026-08-02 Decisions Log entry for what
> the R2011a model is and, importantly, what it is not.

**Decision (2026-07-02): the Sept 1 launch does NOT wait on MathWorks/Simscape licensing being
resolved.** MATLAB/Simscape acquisition is tracked as its own item (see Decisions Log) with its
own timeline; it is explicitly NOT allowed to become a blocker on the MVP ship date.

**Why this works with zero architecture change:** `WorldPackageExporter.cs` already has exactly
the mechanism needed — `WritePendulum`'s `simResultsCsv` parameter falls back to
`GenerateSmallAngleFallback()` (an analytic motion curve) when no real Simscape-exported CSV
exists. The UE-side reader (`DwmGameInstance`/`ADwmPendulumActor`) reads `SimSamples` from the
package and has no idea, and no need to know, whether the data came from Simscape or an
analytic/hand-tuned curve.

> **⚑ CORRECTED 2026-08-02 — this paragraph previously ended "Same pattern applies to the
> turbine mechanism." That is now WRONG and is withdrawn.** The turbine does NOT reuse
> `WritePendulum`'s silent-fallback pattern. `WriteTurbine` **throws** when the CSV is missing,
> and reaches a placeholder only via an explicit `allowFallback: true`. The reason is specific
> to what a turbine looks like: a pendulum on the analytic curve still visibly swings, so nobody
> is fooled, whereas **a turbine on a constant-rate curve is indistinguishable on screen from a
> turbine on real model output** — a rotor turning steadily looks the same either way. The
> failure would be invisible at precisely the place anyone would check for it, and the MVP's
> engineering-rigor claim now rests on that data being model output. What DOES still hold from
> this paragraph is the part that matters: the UE reader consumes `SimSamples` and neither knows
> nor needs to know where the numbers came from. That is why the R2011a model could be dropped
> in without touching anything UE-side.

**The plan:**
- **If Simscape Multibody is licensed and the real CAD-linked turbine model is verified through
  the gate before Week 7 — ship with real Simscape physics data**, exactly as originally planned.
- **If not — ship Week 7 with the turbine driven by a hand-tuned or analytic motion curve**
  (same fallback mechanism, extended from the pendulum to the turbine's actual motion profile).
  The Verification Gate ceremony and ledger/economy behavior are IDENTICAL either way; only the
  data source behind `SimSamples` differs. Definition-of-Done item 5 above is satisfied by either.
- **Swapping in real Simscape data later is a fast-follow, not a re-launch.** Once Simscape is
  licensed, re-run the real pipeline, export the real CSV, and replace the fallback data in a
  routine content update — no UE code changes, no re-architecture, no schedule risk to anything
  already shipped.
- **Be honest in the campaign/demo materials about which data source is live** at time of
  recording — don't imply real CAD-verified physics if shipping on the fallback curve. The
  engineering-rigor thesis is still demonstrated by the PIPELINE and the GATE (the promotion
  ceremony, the verification discipline) even when the underlying motion data is provisional.

## Architectural Fragility Audit — 2026-08-02

> **Why this exists.** DWM_Dev is intended to support the design and development of
> virtually any engineering system, not just the MVP wind turbine. This audit names
> the features that will break first when a system arrives that is not a turbine, and
> states the assumption each one is currently making. Nothing here is a bug today —
> every item works correctly for the turbine. They are recorded because they are
> obvious now and invisible in six months.
>
> **Method:** evidence-based, not from memory. Every claim below was checked against
> the source at the cited location on 2026-08-02. Findings are ranked by likelihood of
> breaking × cost when it does.

| # | Feature | Where | Assumption it makes | What breaks it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `SimSamples` — the mechanism motion format | `WorldPackageExporter.cs` CreateSchema | A mechanism's state is a set of independent SCALAR degrees of freedom on a shared timeline, whose meanings are known out of band. Sub-assumptions: one DOF per part; `Time` is a floating-point primary key; the shared timebase is never declared; channel meaning lives in prose. Note `Parameters` has a `Unit` column and `SimSamples` does not — the table carrying the numbers people actually look at is the one with no units. | Any part with more than one DOF (a tiltrotor nacelle is 6-DOF), or any system with no position at all (thermal, electrical). The turbine only fits because each part is one DOF and `block_power` could be smuggled in as `BlockType = 'Signal'` using Position/Velocity as two generic slots — a documented reuse, but also the schema admitting it has no concept of "a channel". |
| 2 | Nothing validates a package | `WorldPackageExporter.cs` + `DWMStudio.WorldPackageCli/Program.cs` | The only producer is this exporter, so a package is well-formed by construction. Evidence: `SchemaVersion` is WRITTEN IN THREE PLACES AND READ IN ZERO (not even asserted in tests); there are NO FOREIGN KEYS anywhere in the mechanism schema, while `economy_schema.sql` opens with `PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON` and constrains every relation; and `verify` lists tables, counts rows and prints three rows each — it cannot fail. | A `SimSamples` row pointing at a `BlockId` with no `Blocks` row inserts cleanly and verifies green. Uncomfortable because "verification gate" is the project's headline claim; the economy half of the same file knows better, and the asymmetry is the tell. |
| 3 | The pipeline is an enum, not data | `DWMStudio/Models/WorldProject.cs`, `WorldDetailViewModel.cs` | Every engineering system passes through exactly SysML → CAD → MATLAB → Co-Sim → Runtime, exactly once, in that order, and a stage is binary complete/incomplete. `Stages[(int)stage]` casts the enum to a list index; the ViewModel exposes five hardcoded properties feeding five hardcoded XAML slots. | Already violated quietly — the MYSTRAN tower modal deck has no stage, and neither would DATCOM, a wind-tunnel dataset, or a hot-press cure study. Adding a stage is an enum change PLUS a list-index invariant PLUS a ViewModel PLUS XAML. Highest ratio of "definitionally wrong for the stated goal" to "cheap to fix now". |
| 4 | `ToolStatusService` — hardcoded, Windows-COM-shaped tool set | `DWMStudio/Services/ToolStatusService.cs` | A tool is a long-lived process that advertises itself over COM or localhost HTTP. Four fixed booleans; MATLAB and UModel detected via `Type.GetTypeFromProgID`, which is Windows-only and answers "is this REGISTERED?" — not running, not licensed, not the right version; Fusion is a fixed 127.0.0.1:18750. | MYSTRAN and Digital DATCOM are batch executables with no COM server, no port and no notion of "connected". They cannot be represented in this model AT ALL — not badly, just not at all. Any future solver is likelier to look like them than like Fusion. |
| 5 | The CSV interchange carries no identity | `WorldPackageExporter.cs` LoadSamplesFromCsv | The only writer is `wtExportSimSamples.m`, so the file's shape needs no checking. Three compounding facts: the header is skipped by "field 0 did not parse as a number" with no check that it SAYS `Time,Position,Velocity`; the `double.TryParse` return values on columns 1 and 2 are DISCARDED, so an unparseable Position silently becomes 0.0; and column order is positional, so a file with Position and Velocity swapped loads without complaint. | Fair for a tracer. Stops being fair the moment a second tool writes one of these — which is exactly what "support any engineering system" means. |
| 6 | Channel discovery by filename convention | `WorldPackageExporter.cs` SeedTurbineChannel | A mechanism's channel set is fixed at compile time and its files are named after it. Discovery substitutes on the last `_rotor` in the path; the four suffixes are C# literals. | A five-bladed rotor, a second turbine, or a tiltrotor's two nacelles needs a CODE CHANGE to be seen at all, and a missing sibling file returns 0 rather than raising. The manifest that should exist is encoded in filenames. |
| 7 | Five communities and ten resources baked in three places | `economy_schema.sql`, `EconomySeeder.cs`, `GoldenEconomyScenario.cs`, plus 16 of 17 test files | The world has these five communities and these ten resources. 143 hardcoded slug occurrences across the C# tree. | Less fragile than it looks — the SCHEMA is generic, only the seed data is fixed. But three independent definitions of one list is three places to drift, and the outstanding `KnownResourceIds()` edit in `DwmGameInstance.cpp` is a FOURTH copy, in a repo the C# tests cannot reach. |
| 8 | Descriptive `Parameters` duplicated from `wtParameters.m` | `WorldPackageExporter.cs` SeedTurbine | Four numbers will not change, or someone will remember. The code comment is already honest about it: "duplication means they can drift, and drift here is silent." | The density fix moved `rho` and `K_opt` with it; nothing in the C# would have noticed if `bladeLength` had moved too. The package would still export, verify and render. |
| 9 | `AssetBindings` fails silently | `WorldPackageExporter.cs` SeedTurbine / SeedTurbineChannel | A binding either resolves or the absence is noticed by eye. The rotor path is still the `REPLACE_ME` placeholder; a wrong path binds nothing with NO error. | There is a deliberate tripwire test for the placeholder, which is the right call — but the underlying property is that the schema has no way to say "this binding must resolve." |

**Recommended order of repair, if any of this is acted on:**

1. **Cheapest, and definitionally wrong for the stated goal:** item 3, the pipeline enum. Fix it before the shape of the UI silently decides which projects feel possible.
2. **Bites hardest:** items 1 and 2 together, via a single `Channels` table declaring `(BlockId, Slot, Meaning, Unit, Frame)`. That gives `SimSamples` the vocabulary it is missing AND gives `verify` something it can actually fail on. Doing either alone leaves half the problem.
3. Items 5 and 6 become cheap once 1 and 2 exist — a declared channel set replaces both the positional CSV parse and the `_rotor` filename convention.

**Explicitly NOT scheduled for MVP.** None of this blocks the MVP demo; the turbine exercises every one of these features successfully. This is a record of what the next system will find, not a defect list.

General rule: CO2 lasers do NOT cut bare metal (10.6 um is highly reflective); fiber lasers do; diodes mark it. Diode arrays have no optical isolator (fiber lasers do), so sustained back-reflection destroys the diodes and focusing optics with no obvious warning. What DOES work on aluminium: (a) marking ANODISED aluminium is excellent and is genuine material removal -- the laser ablates the dye and anodic layer, 5-25 um deep, crisp bright-on-dark, low power so back-reflection is a non-issue; (b) marking BARE aluminium needs a compound (CerMark / moly spray), which fuses TO the surface and is therefore an additive bonded layer, not a recess; (c) for REAL DEPTH in bare aluminium, use the laser as a RESIST MASK -- coat the stock (paint, vinyl, toner), laser-ablate the pattern through the coating (burning paint, not metal, so reflectivity is irrelevant), chemically etch the exposed metal with sodium hydroxide, then strip the resist.
